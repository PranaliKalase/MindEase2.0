# 🔔 Notification System Specification

## 1. Notification Philosophy

Notifications in MindEase are designed to **inform without overwhelming**. There are NO spam push notifications or artificial urge triggers.

### Notification Categories:

1. **Replies & Interactions**:
   - `CalmSoul21 replied to your post in 📚 Book Club`
   - `Someone found your thought Helpful in #Mindfulness`
2. **Buddy System Updates**:
   - `Your Wellness Buddy check-in is scheduled for 7:00 PM`
   - `New private message from your Peer Buddy`
3. **Club Updates**:
   - `New Weekly Reading Prompt posted in 📚 Book Club`
   - `New Community Playlist added in 🎵 Music Club`
4. **Moderation & Safety Notices**:
   - `Your post was reviewed and published by AI Safety`
5. **Wellness Reminders**:
   - `Daily Reflection Question is ready`
   - `Gentle 2-minute Breathing Exercise reminder`

---

## 2. Controls & Preferences

- **Quiet Hours**: Users can set automatic quiet hours (e.g. 10:00 PM - 8:00 AM) during which all notifications are silenced.
- **Grouped Digest**: Multiple reactions or replies are bundled into a single notification (`"3 people related to your post"`).
- **In-App Drawer**: Notifications appear in a clean glass popover when clicking the 🔔 bell icon in the top navigation.
