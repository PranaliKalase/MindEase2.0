# 👤 Anonymous Profile Architecture

## 1. Privacy-First Identity Model

MindEase profiles prioritize psychological safety. Users operate under pseudonymous personas. Real names, email addresses, and location data are strictly hidden from public profiles.

### Profile Components:

- **Avatar**: Choice of 12 calm, nature-inspired pseudonymous avatars (e.g. Lotus, Forest Mist, Peaceful Ocean, Celestial Star).
- **Nickname**: Customizable handle e.g. `CalmSoul21`, `MountainMist`, `HopefulHeart`.
- **Bio**: Short personal wellness intention (max 160 chars) e.g. *"Learning to practice daily gratitude and slow down."*
- **Interests**: Selected tags e.g. `#Mindfulness`, `#BookClub`, `#Yoga`.
- **Joined Clubs**: Displayed list of user's active clubs.
- **Helpful Contributions**: Total supportive reactions received (e.g. `42 People Helped`).
- **Achievements**: Non-gamified badges celebrating consistency (e.g. `7-Day Reflection Streak`, `Empathetic Listener`).

---

## 2. Strictly Excluded Metrics (Zero Popularity Traps)

- ❌ NO Follower Counts
- ❌ NO Following Counts
- ❌ NO Account Creation Date Pressure
- ❌ NO Influencer Badges
- ❌ NO Activity Leaderboards
