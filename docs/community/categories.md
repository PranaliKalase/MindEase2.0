# 🌿 Clubs & Topic Categories Specification

## 1. Primary Hobby Clubs

Hobby Clubs are first-class feature anchors in the MindEase Sanctuary. They provide structured, positive environments where healing occurs through shared passions.

### 📚 Book Club
- **Weekly Reading Prompt**: *"What book has positively changed your mindset?"*
- **Features**: Book discussions, recommendations, monthly reading challenges, live text-based reading circles.

### 🎵 Music Club
- **Weekly Prompt**: *"Share one song that helps you relax."*
- **Features**: Community relaxation playlists, song recommendations, music therapy discussions, mood-based music sharing.

### 📸 Photography Club
- **Weekly Challenge**: *"Capture something that gives you peace."*
- **Features**: Photo sharing, community gallery, photography discussions, peaceful moment challenges.

### 🧘 Yoga Club
- **Today's Practice**: *"Beginner Yoga Session - 15 Min Relaxation"*
- **Features**: Daily yoga challenges, guided pose discussions, stretching tips, mindfulness exercises.
