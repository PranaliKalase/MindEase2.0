# 🛡️ Moderator Dashboard & Telemetry Specification

## 1. Staff Moderation Interface

The Moderator Dashboard provides human mental health peer moderators with tools to maintain sanctuary safety.

### Dashboard Sections:

1. **Review Queue**:
   - Posts flagged by AI Safety (High Risk 0.61 - 0.85).
   - Posts reported by 3+ community members.
   - Quick action buttons: `Approve`, `Approve with Content Warning`, `Reject Post`, `Escalate to Safety Lead`.
2. **Community Health Telemetry**:
   - Real-time Kindness Score trend graph.
   - Active posts per club (📚 Book, 🎵 Music, 📸 Photography, 🧘 Yoga).
   - AI pipeline classification breakdown.
3. **Audit Log**: Immutable log recording moderator review actions for accountability.
